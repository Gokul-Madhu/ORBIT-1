Starting WebGL

Look inside a virtual rocket and see how its battery, computers, and sensors work together. Everything here runs in simulation. The rocket’s shape and climb are illustrative. The orbit is calculated separately at 400 km above Earth.

New here? Start with the motion sensor
  1. Find it. Click . This sensor tells the computer how the rocket’s motion is changing.
  2. Watch it work. Click Run / resume. The graph shows readings over time. Drag the 3D view to turn it, or scroll to zoom.
  3. See what breaks. Click Pause, then Inject and Step 250 ms (one quarter of a second). The sensor turns red because its message didn’t reach the computer.
  4. Fix it. Click Recover selected fault and step again. The sensor turns green when readings return. If the mission has ended, click Reset to try again.

      Payload is the message written as raw bytes. CRC is a check for changed bytes. Wire ms is the estimated transfer time in milliseconds. Rate bps is the number of bits the connection can send per second.
